About me

I am an ELLIS PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Copenhagen and a member of the FoRML group, supervised by Amartya Sanyal and Michal Valko. My research interests lie in the theoretical foundations of modern trustworthy machine learning.

Previously, I worked on the development of a non-invasive glucose monitoring device at Liom and on improving supply chain operations at Chanel using machine learning.

Prior to that, I obtained an MSc in Statistics from ETH Zurich with a thesis on Meta Feature Learning under the supervision of Andreas Krause, and a BSc in Applied Mathematics from the Polytechnic University of Turin, where I was supported by the Young Talents scholarship.
